# Archive — rejected active-switch design (R1)

`d-hita_rf_frontend_VISUAL_R1_REJECTED.png` is a render of the **first** RF frontend concept: a rigid enclosure holding an SKY13350 RF switch, ESP32-C3 MCU, TP4056 Li-Po charger, TPS63030 regulator, Li-Po battery and USB-C port on Rogers 4350B.

**This design was rejected on 2026-09-28 and must not be built.** It was replaced by the passive dual-feed Kapton-flex design (R2): no switch, MCU, battery, firmware or enclosure. Reasons are in `../../../docs/DECISION_LOG.md`, and the full list of removed parts is in `../../d-hita_rf_frontend_CONFIG.json` under `removedFromPriorRevision`.

Several labels in the image are also garbled or illegible, which is another reason not to use it in the submission.
